## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> # Read-back of `B-P023.lean`
>
> ## Ambient setting
>
> Fix a type `S` of **sorts**. `A : SSet S` is a family of types `A s` (a sorted set); `Sub A` is the family of sorted subsets `L s ⊆ A s`; `SortedEqv A` is the family of sorted equivalences (one equivalence relation per carrier). `Signature S` is a many-sorted signature and `Alg Sig` a sorted algebra. `IsElemTranslation Sig A t s T` says `T : A.1 t → A.1 s` is an elementary translation; `TlGen Sig A t s T` is the inductive closure of identities, elementary translations, and composites. `sortedEqvLe Φ Ψ` means "`Φ` is contained in `Ψ`" (every pair related by `Φ` is related by `Ψ`).
>
> Relevant background notions used here:
>
> - `charEqv L`, for `L : Sub A`, is the sorted equivalence at sort `s` relating `x,y` iff `x ∈ L s ↔ y ∈ L s` (same membership status).
> - `IsCongruence Sig A.2 Φ` means `Φ` is a congruence of the algebra `A`.
> - `congCogenerated Sig A L` is the greatest congruence contained in `charEqv L`.
> - `sat Φ X : Sub A` is the **saturation** of `X` by `Φ`: at sort `s`, `sat Φ X s` is the set of `y` such that there exists `x ∈ X s` with `Φ s x y`.
> - `IsSat Φ X` is the assertion that `X` is **`Φ`-saturated**, i.e. `sat Φ X = X` (equivalently: `X` is a union of `Φ`-classes).
>
> ## Theorems
>
> ### `isSat_iff_sortedEqvLe_charEqv`
> For any sorted equivalence `Φ` and sorted subset `L`,
>
> > `IsSat Φ L ↔ sortedEqvLe Φ (charEqv L)`.
>
> In words: `L` is saturated under `Φ` **iff** `Φ` is contained in `charEqv L`, i.e. whenever `x ~_Φ y`, the elements `x` and `y` have the same membership status in `L` (both in or both out, at the relevant sort).
>
> Proof sketch of direction `→`: from `sat Φ L = L`, a `Φ`-related pair `x ~ y` with `x` (resp. `y`) in `L` shows the other one lies in `sat Φ L` and hence in `L`; gives the biconditional. Direction `←`: if `Φ` is below `charEqv L`, then any `y ∈ sat Φ L s` comes from some `x ∈ L s` with `x ~ y`, and the biconditional forces `y ∈ L s`; conversely each `a ∈ L s` is in `sat Φ L s` via reflexivity. So `sat Φ L = L`.
>
> ### `isSat_iff_le_congCogenerated`
> For a signature `Sig`, algebra `A`, subset `L : Sub A.1`, and a sorted equivalence `Φ` that is a congruence,
>
> > `IsSat Φ L ↔ sortedEqvLe Φ (congCogenerated Sig A L)`.
>
> In words: a **congruence** `Φ` saturates `L` iff `Φ` is contained in the congruence cogenerated by `L`.
>
> Proof: rewrite the left side by the previous theorem to `Φ ≤ charEqv L`; then the forward implication is exactly the universal property of `congCogenerated` (every congruence below `charEqv L` is below the cogenerated one), and the backward implication composes `Φ ≤ congCogenerated ≤ charEqv L`.
>
