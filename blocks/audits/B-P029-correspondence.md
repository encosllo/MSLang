## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> # Read-back of `B-P029.lean`
>
> ## Ambient setting
>
> Fix a type `S` of **sorts**. `A : SSet S` is a sorted set (family of carriers `A s`); `Sub A` is the family of sorted subsets; `SortedEqv A` is the family of sorted equivalences. `Sig` is a many-sorted signature; `Alg Sig` a sorted algebra. `IsElemTranslation Sig A t s T` marks an elementary translation; `TlGen Sig A t s T` is the inductive closure of identities, elementary translations, and composites. `congCogenerated Sig A L` is the congruence cogenerated by a subset `L`, and `eqvClass Φ s a` is the `Φ`-class of `a` in `A s`.
>
> ## Definitions
>
> ### `cogClassSets`
> For signature `Sig`, algebra `A`, subset `L : Sub A.1`, sort `t`, and `a : A.1 t`, `cogClassSets Sig A L t a` is the **set of subsets of `A t`** consisting of all
>
> > `X = T ⁻¹' (L s)` for which there exist a sort `s` and a translation `T : A t → A s` with `TlGen Sig A t s T`, such that additionally `T a ∈ L s`.
>
> So each member is the preimage of `L` along a translation that **sends `a` into `L`**.
>
> ### `cogClassSetsCompl`
> `cogClassSetsCompl Sig A L t a` is the analogous set of subsets `X = T ⁻¹' (L s)` over translations `T : A t → A s` for which `T a ∉ L s` (the translation sends `a` **out of** `L`).
>
> ## Theorem
>
> ### `eqvClass_congCogenerated`
> For every sort `t` and element `a : A.1 t`,
>
> > `eqvClass (congCogenerated Sig A L) t a
> >   = (⋂₀ cogClassSets Sig A L t a) \ (⋃₀ cogClassSetsCompl Sig A L t a)`.
>
> Here `⋂₀` is the intersection of a set of sets and `⋃₀` the union of a set of sets.
>
> In words: the equivalence class of `a` under the cogenerated congruence is exactly
>
> > **(the intersection of all translation-preimages of `L` that contain `a`) minus (the union of all translation-preimages of `L` that exclude `a`)**.
>
> Explicitly, `b` lies in the class of `a` iff:
> 1. `b` lies in `T ⁻¹' (L s)` for every translation `T : A t → A s` with `T a ∈ L s`; and
> 2. `b` lies in **no** `T ⁻¹' (L s)` for a translation `T` with `T a ∉ L s`.
>
> Equivalently: for every sort `s` and every translation `T : A t → A s`, one has `T a ∈ L s ↔ T b ∈ L s`. (This is precisely the defining relation of the cogenerated congruence.)
>
> Proof sketch.
> - (⊆) If `b` is `a`-equivalent, take any `X = T ⁻¹' (L s)` in `cogClassSets` with `T a ∈ L s`; the equivalence gives `T b ∈ L s`, so `b ∈ X`; hence `b` is in the intersection. If `X = T ⁻¹' (L s)` is in `cogClassSetsCompl` with `T a ∉ L s`, the equivalence gives `T b ∉ L s`, so `b ∉ X`; hence `b` is outside the union.
> - (⊇) Conversely, suppose `b` is in the intersection and outside the union; unwind to the pointwise statement `∀ s T, TlGen t s T → (T a ∈ L s ↔ T b ∈ L s)`. The forward direction follows from the intersection membership; the backward direction is proved by contradiction: if `T a ∉ L s` while `T b ∈ L s`, then `T ⁻¹' (L s)` would be a member of `cogClassSetsCompl` containing `b`, contradicting `b ∉ ⋃₀ cogClassSetsCompl`.
>
