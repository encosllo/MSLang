## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> # Read-back of `B-P025.lean`
>
> ## Ambient setting
>
> Fix a type `S` of **sorts**. `A : SSet S` is a sorted set (family of carriers `A s`); `Sub A` is the family of sorted subsets; `SortedEqv A` is the family of sorted equivalences. `Sig` is a many-sorted signature and `Alg Sig` a sorted algebra. `TlGen Sig A t s T` is the inductive family of **translations** `T : A.1 t → A.1 s`, generated by identities (`TlGen.refl`), elementary translations (`TlGen.elem`, via `IsElemTranslation`), and composition (`TlGen.comp`).
>
> - `congCogenerated Sig A L` is the congruence cogenerated by the subset `L` (the greatest congruence contained in `charEqv L`).
> - `complA X : Sub A` is the sortwise **complement**: `(complA X) s` is the complement of `X s` in `A s`.
>
> ## Theorem
>
> ### `congCogenerated_compl`
> For every signature `Sig`, algebra `A`, and sorted subset `L : Sub A.1`,
>
> > `congCogenerated Sig A L = congCogenerated Sig A (complA L)`.
>
> In words: **cogenerating from a subset gives the same congruence as cogenerating from its complement.** The cogenerated congruence depends only on the equivalence "same membership status in `L`", and complementing `L` does not change which pairs have equal membership status.
>
> Proof sketch. Equality of sorted equivalences is shown sort by sort and pair by pair. For a translation `T` out of the current sort, membership of `T x` in `L s` is the negation of membership of `T x` in `(complA L) s`, and likewise for `T y`; hence the two biconditionals `T x ∈ L s ↔ T y ∈ L s` and `T x ∈ (complA L) s ↔ T y ∈ (complA L) s` are equivalent (double negation).
>
> ## Declared (bodyless) background
>
> `IsElemTranslation`, `SSet`, `Signature`, `SortedEqv`, `Sub`, `TlGen`, `complA`, `congCogenerated`. (The proof also invokes an auxiliary "bridge" fact, not declared in this file, relating membership in `complA L` to negation of membership in `L`.)
