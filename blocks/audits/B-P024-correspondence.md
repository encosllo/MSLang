## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> # Read-back of `B-P024.lean`
>
> ## Ambient setting
>
> Fix a type `S` of **sorts**. `A : SSet S` is a sorted set (family of carriers `A s`); `Alg Sig` is a sorted algebra for a many-sorted signature `Sig`. `IsCongruence Sig A.2 Φ` says the sorted equivalence `Φ` is a congruence of `A`. `congCogenerated Sig A L` is the greatest congruence contained in `charEqv L`. `ClosesUnderEtl`/`ClosesUnderTl` express compatibility of `Φ` with elementary translations / all translations (`TlGen` is the closure of identities and elementary translations under composition).
>
> ## Key auxiliary definitions
>
> ### `deltaSub s Y`
> `deltaSub s Y : Sub A` is the sorted subset concentrated at a single sort: it equals `Y` at sort `s` and the empty set at every other sort. (Written in the proofs as `Function.update (fun _ => ∅) s Y`, whence `deltaSub s Y s = Y` and `deltaSub s Y s' = ∅` for `s' ≠ s`.)
>
> ### `eqvClass Φ s a`
> `eqvClass Φ s a : Set (A s)` is the `Φ`-**equivalence class** of `a` in `A s`, i.e. the set of `b` with `Φ s a b`.
>
> ### `sortedEqv_iInf`
> For a family `Φ : ι → SortedEqv A`, `sortedEqv_iInf Φ` is the pointwise **intersection** (greatest lower bound): at sort `s`, `x` and `y` are related iff `Φ i s x y` for all `i`.
>
> ## Theorem
>
> ### `isCongruence_eq_iInf_congCogenerated`
> For every congruence `Φ` of the algebra `A`,
>
> > `Φ = sortedEqv_iInf (fun p : Sigma A.1 => congCogenerated Sig A (deltaSub p.1 (eqvClass Φ p.1 p.2)))`.
>
> Here `Sigma A.1` is the disjoint union of the carriers, i.e. the type of pairs `(s, a)` with `a : A s`; `p.1` is the sort and `p.2` the element.
>
> In words: **every congruence is the intersection (meet) of the congruences cogenerated by the individual equivalence classes.** For each element `a` of `A` at sort `s`, form the subset which equals the `Φ`-class of `a` at sort `s` and is empty at all other sorts, and take its cogenerated congruence; then intersect these over all `(s, a)`; the result is exactly `Φ`.
>
> Equivalently, pointwise: `Φ t x y` holds iff for every `(s, a)` and every translation `T` out of sort `t`, `T x` and `T y` have the same membership status in the subset `deltaSub s (eqvClass Φ s a)`.
>
> Proof sketch. (`⊆`) If `x ~_Φ y`, for any `p = (s, a)` and any translation `T : A t → A s` (constant on sorts other than `s`), closure of `Φ` under translations gives `T x ~_Φ T y`; transitivity of `Φ` at `s` yields that `T x` lies in the class of `a` iff `T y` does (and off sort `s` the target subset is empty, so the condition is trivial). (`⊇`) Evaluating the intersection at `p = (t, x)` with the identity translation gives that `y` lies in the `Φ`-class of `x`, i.e. `x ~_Φ y`.
>
