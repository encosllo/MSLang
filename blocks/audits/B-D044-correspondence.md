> ## Translations
>
> - `IsElemTranslation Sig A t s T`, for `t s : S` and `T : A.1 t → A.1 s`:
>   there exist a word `w : List S`, a position `i : Fin w.length` with
>   `hwit : w.get i = t`, an operation symbol `σ : Sig (w, s)` (arity `(w, s)`),
>   and a family `a` giving a value of sort `w.get k` for every `k ≠ i`, such that
>   for every `x : A.1 t`,
>   `T x = A.2 (w, s) σ (fun k => if k = i then x else a k ·)`
>   — with the `i`-th argument placed at the equation `w.get i = t` by casting
>   (the transport `(congrArg A.1 (…)).symm ▸ x`) and the other positions filled by
>   `a`. So `T` is the unary operation "apply `σ` with the `i`-th slot left as the
>   variable and all other slots fixed". An **elementary (one-step) translation**.
> - `TlGen Sig A`, an inductive proposition indexed by `(t s : S)` and
>   `(A.1 t → A.1 s)`, the smallest relation containing:
>   - `refl t : TlGen Sig A t t id` (identity maps);
>   - `elem` : any `IsElemTranslation Sig A t s T` gives `TlGen Sig A t s T`;
>   - `comp` : `TlGen Sig A t u T` and `TlGen Sig A u s U` give
>     `TlGen Sig A t s (U ∘ T)` (closure under composition).
>   Thus `TlGen` is the set of translations generated by the elementary
>   translations and identities — the translation semigroup/monoid of the algebra
>   (unary term operations).
>
> ## Congruence cogenerated by a language
>
> - `congCogenerated Sig A L : SortedEqv A.1`, for `L : Sub A.1`. At sort `t` its
>   relation is
>   `x ~ y  ⟺  ∀ (s : S) (T : A.1 t → A.1 s), TlGen Sig A t s T → (T x ∈ L s ↔ T y ∈ L s)`,
>   i.e. `x` and `y` are related iff **no generated translation** `T` maps one into
>   the language `L` and the other out of it (at any target sort). The setoid
>   structure is supplied: reflexivity is `Iff.rfl`, symmetry reverses the iff, and
>   transitivity composes the iff's. This is the **syntactic / cogenerated
>   congruence** of `L`.
>
> ## Regular languages
>
> - `IsRegularLanguage Sig A L := IsFiniteIndex (congCogenerated Sig A L)`: `L` is
>   regular iff its syntactic congruence has finite index (finitely many classes
>   overall).
> - `regularLanguages Sig A := {L : Sub A.1 | IsRegularLanguage Sig A L}`: the set
>   of regular sortwise subsets of `A`.
>
