## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> # Read-back of `B-R021.lean`
>
> ## Ambient setting
>
> Fix a type `S` of **sorts**. `SSet S` is the type of sorted sets (families of carriers indexed by `S`). `SortedMap A B` is a family of functions `f s : A s → B s`. `Sub A` is the family of sorted subsets. `SortedEqv A` is the family of sorted equivalences (one equivalence relation per carrier). `Sig` is a many-sorted signature; `Alg Sig` is a sorted algebra; `AlgStruct Sig A` is the algebra structure on `A`. `IsAlgHom Sig FA FB f` says the sorted map `f` is an algebra homomorphism. `IsElemTranslation`/`TlGen` denote elementary translations and their closure. `congCogenerated Sig A L` is the congruence cogenerated by a subset `L`. `inverseImage f Y` is the sortwise preimage; `pullbackEqv f Ψ` transports a sorted equivalence back along `f`. `sortedEqvLe Φ Ψ` means `Φ` is contained in `Ψ`.
>
> ## Homomorphism facts
>
> ### `isAlgHom_id`
> For any algebra structure `FA` on `A`, the pointwise identity map `fun _ => id` is an algebra homomorphism `A → A`.
>
> ### `isAlgHom_comp`
> If `f : SortedMap A B` and `g : SortedMap B C` are algebra homomorphisms (`hf`, `hg`), then the pointwise composite `fun s => g s ∘ f s` is an algebra homomorphism `A → C`.
>
> ## Order structure on sorted equivalences
>
> ### `sortedEqvLe_antisymm`
> If `sortedEqvLe Φ Ψ` and `sortedEqvLe Ψ Φ`, then `Φ = Ψ`. In words: containment of sorted equivalences is antisymmetric, so `sortedEqvLe` is a **partial order** (a preorder that is antisymmetric). Proof: sort by sort, the two directions give logical equivalence of the relations, hence equality of the `Setoid`s.
>
> ## Bundled surjective homomorphisms ("epis")
>
> ### `structure AlgEpi`
> `AlgEpi Sig A B` bundles a sorted map `map : SortedMap A.1 B.1`, a proof `hom` that it is an algebra homomorphism, and a proof `surj : ∀ s, Function.Surjective (map s)` that it is sortwise surjective.
>
> ### `AlgEpiId`, `AlgEpiComp`
> - `AlgEpiId Sig A : AlgEpi Sig A A` is the identity epi (identity map, `isAlgHom_id`, `Function.surjective_id`).
> - `AlgEpiComp Sig g f : AlgEpi Sig A C` for `g : AlgEpi Sig B C`, `f : AlgEpi Sig A B` has underlying map `fun s => g.map s ∘ f.map s`; the homomorphism property is `isAlgHom_comp`, and surjectivity is the composite of the two surjections.
>
> ### `AlgEpiExt`
> For `f, g : AlgEpi Sig A B`, if their underlying maps are equal (`f.map = g.map`), then `f = g`. So an epi is determined by its underlying sorted map.
>
> ### `AlgEpiComp_id_left`, `AlgEpiComp_id_right`, `AlgEpiComp_assoc`
> - `AlgEpiComp (AlgEpiId B) f = f` (left identity),
> - `AlgEpiComp f (AlgEpiId A) = f` (right identity),
> - `AlgEpiComp (AlgEpiComp h g) f = AlgEpiComp h (AlgEpiComp g f)` (associativity) for `h : AlgEpi Sig C D`, `g : AlgEpi Sig B C`, `f : AlgEpi Sig A B`.
>
> Together with the previous facts, surjective algebra homomorphisms form a **category** (objects: algebras, morphisms: epis).
>
> ## Contravariant actions
>
> ### `SubMap`
> For an epi `f : AlgEpi Sig A B`, `SubMap Sig f : Sub B.1 → Sub A.1` is the sortwise inverse image `inverseImage f.map`. So it maps subsets of the target back to subsets of the source (contravariant).
>
> ### `CgrMap`
> For an epi `f : AlgEpi Sig A B`, `CgrMap Sig f : SortedEqv B.1 → SortedEqv A.1` is `pullbackEqv f.map`: it transports a sorted equivalence on `B` back to one on `A` (contravariant).
>
> ### Functoriality
> - `SubMap_id`: `SubMap Sig (AlgEpiId Sig A) = id`.
> - `SubMap_comp`: `SubMap Sig (AlgEpiComp Sig g f) = SubMap Sig f ∘ SubMap Sig g`.
> - `CgrMap_id`: `CgrMap Sig (AlgEpiId Sig A) = id`.
> - `CgrMap_comp`: `CgrMap Sig (AlgEpiComp Sig g f) = CgrMap Sig f ∘ CgrMap Sig g`.
>
> Thus `SubMap` and `CgrMap` are **contravariant functors** from the category of algebras and epis: identity maps act as identities, and composition reverses order. (All four are proved componentwise, essentially by definition.)
>
> ## Naturality of cogeneration
>
> ### `congCogenerated_natural`
> For an epi `f : AlgEpi Sig A B` and subset `M : Sub B.1`,
>
> > `CgrMap Sig f (congCogenerated Sig B M) = congCogenerated Sig A (SubMap Sig f M)`.
>
> In words: **pulling back the congruence cogenerated on `B` by `M` gives exactly the congruence cogenerated on `A` by the inverse image of `M`.** Combined with the fact that `CgrMap f` is pullback along `f`, this says `congCogenerated` is natural with respect to epimorphisms.
>
> Proof: antisymmetry of `sortedEqvLe` applied to the two inequalities `pullbackEqv f (congCogenerated B M) ≤ congCogenerated A (inverseImage f M)` (from `pullbackEqv_congCogenerated_le`) and `congCogenerated A (inverseImage f M) ≤ pullbackEqv f (congCogenerated B M)` (from `congCogenerated_le_pullback_of_surj`, using surjectivity of the epi).
>
