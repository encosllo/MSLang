> ## Main proved theorems
>
> 1. **`langFormationOf_inverseImage`.** Let `G` be a congruence formation, `A B` sorted
>    sets, `M : Sub (Term Sig B)` with `M ∈ langFormationOf Sig G B`, and
>    `f : SortedMap (Term Sig A) (Term Sig B)` an algebra homomorphism of the term
>    algebras. Assume that the composite
>    `prAlg (congCogenerated M) ∘ f : Term Sig A → Term Sig B / congCogenerated M` is
>    **surjective in every sort** (the `hsurj` hypothesis). Then the preimage
>    `inverseImage f M` lies in `langFormationOf Sig G A`.
>
>    So: **language formations are closed under inverse images along (this class of)
>    surjective homomorphisms.** The proof factors `f` through the quotient by the
>    congruence cogenerated by `M`, obtains a kernel inclusion via
>    `pullbackEqv_congCogenerated_le`, and applies `langFormationOf_ker` and
>    `isSat_iff_le_congCogenerated`.
>
> 2. **`langFormationOf_ker`.** Let `G` be a congruence formation, `A B` sorted sets,
>    `M : Sub (Term Sig B)` with `M ∈ langFormationOf Sig G B`, and `f : SortedMap
>    (Term Sig A) (Term Sig B)` an algebra homomorphism whose composite with the
>    projection `prAlg (congCogenerated M)` is surjective in every sort. Then for every
>    subfamily `N : Sub (Term Sig A)`, if `N` is saturated by the kernel of that
>    composite `(s ↦ prAlg … s ∘ f s)`, then `N ∈ langFormationOf Sig G A`.
>
>    So: any language saturated by the kernel of the surjection onto the quotient by
>    `congCogenerated M` belongs to the language formation.
>

> # B-C010 verdict

> ## Clause-by-clause
>
> Contract clause 1: "Let $A$ and $B$ be two $S$-sorted sets". Lean counterpart:

>

> `M ∈ langFormationOf Sig G B`. Match.
>

> $\mathbf{T}_{\Sigma}(A)$ to $\mathbf{T}_{\Sigma}(B)$". Lean counterpart: `f` is an
> algebra homomorphism `Term Sig A → Term Sig B` whose composite with
> `prAlg (congCogenerated M)` is surjective in every sort (`hsurj`). This is exactly the
> condition spelled out in the contract's commented line
> ("If $\mathrm{pr}^{\Omega^{\mathbf{T}_{\Sigma}(B)}(M)}\circ f$ is an epimorphism"),
> i.e. the defining content of an $\Omega(M)$-epimorphism; homomorphism-ness and
> surjectivity-in-every-sort are the categorical epimorphism conditions for sorted
> algebras. Match.
>
> Contract clause 4: "Then $f^{-1}[M]\in\mathcal{L}_{\mathfrak{F}}(A)$". Lean
> counterpart: conclusion `inverseImage f M ∈ langFormationOf Sig G A`, where
> `inverseImage f M = (s ↦ f s ⁻¹' M s)`. Match.
>
> The supporting lemma `pullbackEqv_congCogenerated_le` and the use of
> `langFormationOf_ker` are proof devices, not contract clauses.
>
> Contract clauses with no Lean counterpart: none
>
> Verdict: equivalent — the Lean statement is exactly the contract's inverse-image
> closure under an $\Omega(M)$-epimorphism, with the epimorphism hypothesis realized as
> surjectivity of the composite with the quotient projection.
