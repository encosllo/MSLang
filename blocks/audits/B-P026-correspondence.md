> - Clause "⋂_{j∈J} Ω^A(L^j) ⊆ Ω^A(⋂_{j∈J} L^j)": matched by `congCogenerated_iInter_le`, i.e. `sortedEqvLe (sortedEqv_iInf (fun j => congCogenerated Sig A (L j))) (congCogenerated Sig A (fun s => ⋂ j, L j s))`, with `sortedEqvLe Φ Ψ := ∀ s x y, (Φ s).r x y → (Ψ s).r x y` (relation inclusion Φ⊆Ψ).

> - Supporting fact that the pointwise intersection is again a congruence: Lean `isCongruence_iInf`.
>
> Contract clauses with no Lean counterpart: none.
>
> Verdict: formal_stronger — the same inclusion is proved without the contract's nonemptiness hypothesis on `J`.
>
> Justification: the inclusion matches exactly, and dropping the nonempty-index requirement makes the Lean statement strictly more general.
