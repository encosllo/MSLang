> # B-P020 verdict

> VERDICT: equivalent
>
> JUSTIFICATION: The manuscript's B-P020 ("Form_Alg(Sigma) and Form_Cgr(Sigma)
> are isomorphic complete lattices") is directly witnessed by Lean item 11,
> formAlgFormCgrIso, an order isomorphism between algebraFormations Sig and
> congruenceFormations Sig with respect to inclusion / pointwise-inclusion
> orders -- exactly the orders the manuscript places on Form_Alg and
> Form_Cgr. An order isomorphism between complete lattices automatically
> preserves arbitrary meets/joins (they are order-definable), so it is an
> adequate, not weaker, witness for "isomorphic complete lattices"; nothing in
> the contract demands more than order-theoretic isomorphism, and the
> underlying completeness of both lattices is established by prior background
> propositions (B-P014, B-C005) rather than being part of B-P020 itself. The
> apparent extra granularity in the Lean development is not additional
> mathematical content: items 3-4 (monotonicity of both directions) and 9-10
> (mutual inverse) are precisely the ingredients that constitute "being an
> order isomorphism," so proving them as separate lemmas before packaging them
> into item 11 is decomposition, not strengthening -- the combined content is
> logically equivalent to asserting the single isomorphism. Item 2
> (algebraFormationOfCongruenceFormation_isAlgebraFormation) and the implicit
> congruenceFormationOf-is-a-congruence-formation fact correspond to the
> manuscript's own separately labeled prerequisite propositions B-P015 and
> B-P019 respectively (both explicitly invoked as hypotheses feeding into
> B-P020's proof), so this mirrors the manuscript's own structure rather than
> exceeding it. The one soft spot is that the read-back describes item 11's
> order as only "presumably" inclusion/pointwise-inclusion and notes it saw
> only the toFun component directly, but since items 7-10 supply exactly the
> missing inverse map, monotonicity, and mutual-inverse data needed to
> complete that OrderIso, this is a read-back visibility limitation rather
> than an actual formalization gap. Net: no hypothesis is dropped, no
> conclusion is inflated relative to the target proposition -- equivalent.
