## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> `ClosesUnderEtl Sig A Φ` = for all `t s`, `x y : A_t`, `T : A_t → A_s`,
> `IsElemTranslation Sig A t s T → (Φ t).r x y → (Φ s).r (T x)(T y)`.
> `ClosesUnderTl` is the same with `TlGen Sig A t s T`.
> `IsCongruence Sig A.2 Φ` is the standard operation-compatibility condition;
> `IsElemTranslation` unpacks as an operation `F_σ` with a variable inserted at
> one position `i` (`w.get i = t`) and constants elsewhere; `TlGen` is the least
> family containing `id`, all elementary translations, and closed under
> composition (constructors `refl`/`elem`/`comp`).
>
> `isCongruence_iff_closesUnderEtl` and `closesUnderEtl_iff_closesUnderTl` are
> `(1) ↔ (2)` and `(2) ↔ (3)`. (1)⇒(2): unpack the elementary translation and
> apply the congruence property to the two tuples differing only at position
> `i`. (2)⇒(1): telescoping — `mixedTuple a b j` interpolates between `a`
> (`j = 0`) and `b` (`j = Fin.last`); each step changes coordinate `j` from its
> `a`-value to its `b`-value via the elementary translation
> `Tj z = F_σ(insertTuple j z c)`, and the steps are chained by transitivity
> over `Fin.induction`. (2)⇒(3): induction on the `TlGen` derivation. (3)⇒(2):
> the `elem` constructor.
