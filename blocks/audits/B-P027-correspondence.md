## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> # Read-back of `B-P027.lean`
>
> ## Ambient setting
>
> Fix a type `S` of **sorts**. `A : SSet S` is a sorted set (family of carriers `A s`); `Sub A` is the family of sorted subsets. `Sig` is a many-sorted signature; `Alg Sig` a sorted algebra. `IsElemTranslation Sig A t s T` marks an elementary translation `T : A.1 t → A.1 s`; `TlGen Sig A t s T` is the inductive closure of identities, elementary translations, and composites. `congCogenerated Sig A L` is the congruence cogenerated by a subset `L`, and `sortedEqvLe Φ Ψ` means `Φ` is contained in `Ψ`.
>
> ## Auxiliary definitions
>
> ### `deltaSub s Y`
> The sorted subset equal to `Y` at sort `s` and empty at every other sort.
>
> ### `transPreimage T L`
> For `T : A t → A s` and `L : Sub A`, the **translation preimage** subset, equal at sort `t` to `T ⁻¹' (L s)` and empty at every other sort (i.e. `deltaSub t (T ⁻¹' L s)`). At sort `t` an element `x` belongs to it exactly when `T x ∈ L s`.
>
> ## Theorem
>
> ### `congCogenerated_le_transPreimage`
> For a translation `T : A.1 t → A.1 s` (hypothesis `hT : TlGen Sig A t s T`) and any subset `L : Sub A.1`,
>
> > `sortedEqvLe (congCogenerated Sig A L)
> >              (congCogenerated Sig A (transPreimage T L))`.
>
> In words: **cogeneration by `L` is contained in cogeneration by the preimage of `L` under `T`.** If `x` and `y` are indistinguishable by `L` under all translations, then they are indistinguishable by the subset `T ⁻¹' L` under all translations.
>
> Precisely, at any sort `t'`: `x ~_cog(L) y` implies `x ~_cog(transPreimage T L) y`. The latter means: for every sort `s''` and every translation `V : A t' → A s''`, `V x ∈ (transPreimage T L) s'' ↔ V y ∈ (transPreimage T L) s''`.
>
> Proof sketch. Given `hxy : x ~_cog(L) y`, a `V` and `hV : TlGen t' s'' V`: if `s'' = t`, the membership in the preimage subset at `s''` unwinds to `(T (V x) ∈ L s) ↔ (T (V y) ∈ L s)`, which is obtained from `hxy` applied to the composite translation `T ∘ V` (legitimate by `TlGen.comp hV hT`). If `s'' ≠ t`, the preimage subset is empty at `s''` and the biconditional is trivial.
>
