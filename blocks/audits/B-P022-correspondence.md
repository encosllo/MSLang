## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> `congCogenerated Sig A L` relates `x y : A_t` iff for every sort `s` and every
> `t`-translation `T : A_t → A_s` (`TlGen Sig A t s T`), `T x ∈ L s ↔ T y ∈ L s`.
> `charEqv L` relates `x y` at `s` iff `x ∈ L s ↔ y ∈ L s` (i.e. `Ker(ch^L)`).
>
> (1) `congCogenerated_isCongruence`: reduce to `ClosesUnderEtl` via `B-P021`;
> the key is that `U ∘ T` is again a translation (`TlGen.comp`), so the "for all
> translations" clause of `congCogenerated` is instantiated at `U ∘ T`.
> (2) `congCogenerated_le_charEqv`: instantiate the universal clause at the
> identity translation (`TlGen.refl`).
> (3) `le_congCogenerated_of_isCongruence`: a congruence `Φ` is closed under
> translations (via `B-P021`), so `(Φ t).r x y` gives `(Φ s).r (T x)(T y)`;
> the hypothesis `Φ ⊆ Ker(ch^L)` then gives `T x ∈ L s ↔ T y ∈ L s`.

## Stage 2 -- comparison (fresh agent, read-back + contract only)

> **Outcome: equivalent.**
