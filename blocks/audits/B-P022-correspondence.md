## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> # Read-back of `B-P022.lean`
>
> ## Ambient setting
>
> Fix a type `S` of **sorts**.
>
> - A **sorted set** `A : SSet S` is a family of types `A s`, one for each sort `s : S`.
> - A **sorted subset** `L : Sub A` is a family of subsets `L s ⊆ A s`.
> - A **sorted equivalence** `Φ : SortedEqv A` is a family of equivalence relations (Lean `Setoid`s), one on each carrier `A s`.
> - `Signature S` is a many-sorted signature; `Alg Sig` is a sorted set together with an interpretation of the signature. `finOp A w s` is the carrier of operation symbols with input word `w : List S` and output sort `s`, and `wordProd A w` is the product of the carriers along the word `w`.
> - `IsElemTranslation Sig A t s T`, for a function `T : A.1 t → A.1 s`, says `T` is an **elementary translation** (a one-variable derived operation).
> - `TlGen Sig A t s T` is the inductively generated family of **translations**, with constructors:
>   - `TlGen.refl t : TlGen Sig A t t id` (identities are translations),
>   - `TlGen.elem`: every elementary translation is a translation,
>   - `TlGen.comp`: from `TlGen Sig A t u T` and `TlGen Sig A u s U` one gets `TlGen Sig A t s (U ∘ T)`.
> - `sortedEqvLe Φ Ψ` reads "`Φ` is contained in `Ψ`": every pair related by `Φ` at a sort is related by `Ψ` there. (This direction is fixed by the usage below: e.g. in `congCogenerated_le_charEqv` the proof instantiates the cogenerated relation to conclude the `charEqv` relation.)
>
> `AlgStruct`, `IsCongruence`, `ClosesUnderEtl`, `ClosesUnderTl`, `sortedEqvLe`, `finOp`, `wordProd` are declared here without bodies and used below.
>
> ## Definitions
>
> ### `charEqv`
> For `L : Sub A`, `charEqv L : SortedEqv A` is **defined** to be the sorted equivalence whose relation at sort `s` is
>
> > `x ~ y` iff `x ∈ L s ↔ y ∈ L s`,
>
> i.e. `x` and `y` have the same membership status in `L s`. (Reflexivity/symmetry/transitivity come from those of `↔`.)
>
> ### Closure and congruence predicates
> - `ClosesUnderEtl Sig A Φ` / `ClosesUnderTl Sig A Φ`: `Φ` is compatible with elementary translations / with all translations: related inputs give related outputs under every such translation.
> - `IsCongruence Sig F Φ`: `Φ` is a congruence of the algebra (compatible with all operations).
>
> ## Theorems and lemmas
>
> 1. `isCongruence_iff_closesUnderEtl`: for a sorted equivalence `Φ`,
>    `IsCongruence Sig A.2 Φ ↔ ClosesUnderEtl Sig A Φ`.
>    (Being a congruence is exactly being closed under elementary translations.)
>
> 2. `closesUnderEtl_of_closesUnderTl`: `ClosesUnderTl Φ → ClosesUnderEtl Φ`.
>
> 3. `closesUnderEtl_of_isCongruence`: `IsCongruence Φ → ClosesUnderEtl Φ`.
>
> 4. `closesUnderTl_of_closesUnderEtl`: `ClosesUnderEtl Φ → ClosesUnderTl Φ`.
>
> 5. `congruence_of_closesUnderEtl`: `ClosesUnderEtl Φ → IsCongruence Φ`.
>
> 6. `congCogenerated Sig A L : SortedEqv A.1` is the **cogenerated** sorted equivalence attached to the subset `L`. Its characterizing property is given by items 7–9.
>
> 7. `congCogenerated_isCongruence`: `congCogenerated Sig A L` is a congruence of `A`.
>    (Proof: via `isCongruence_iff_closesUnderEtl`, reducing to closure under translations, which follows because translations are generated by elementary ones.)
>
> 8. `congCogenerated_le_charEqv`: `sortedEqvLe (congCogenerated Sig A L) (charEqv L)`, i.e. the cogenerated relation is **contained in** `charEqv L`. Concretely: if `x,y` are cogenerated-equivalent at sort `t`, then `x ∈ L t ↔ y ∈ L t`. (Proof instantiates the translation slot with the identity `id` at `t`.)
>
> 9. `le_congCogenerated_of_isCongruence`: for any sorted equivalence `Φ`, if
>    - `Φ` is a congruence, and
>    - `sortedEqvLe Φ (charEqv L)` (Φ is contained in `charEqv L`),
>    then `sortedEqvLe Φ (congCogenerated Sig A L)`.
>
>    Items 7–9 together say: **`congCogenerated Sig A L` is the greatest congruence contained in `charEqv L`** — it is a congruence (7), it lies below `charEqv L` (8), and every congruence lying below `charEqv L` lies below it (9).
