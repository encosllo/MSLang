## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> `pullbackEqv f Ψ` relates `x y` at `s` iff `(Ψ s).r (f s x) (f s y)` (the
> pullback `(f×f)⁻¹[Ψ]`); `inverseImage f M s = f s ⁻¹' M s` (`f⁻¹[M]`).
> `pullbackEqv_congCogenerated_le hf M` gives `(f×f)⁻¹[Ω^B(M)] ⊆ Ω^A(f⁻¹[M])`
> for `hf : IsAlgHom`; `congCogenerated_le_pullback_of_surj hf hsurj M` gives the
> reverse inclusion for `f` a componentwise-surjective homomorphism.
> Helpers: `tlGen_map` transports an `A`-translation `V` along `f` to a
> `B`-translation `U` with `U (f z) = f (V z)`; `tlGen_lift` lifts a
> `B`-translation `U` along an epimorphism to `V` with `f (V z) = U (f z)`
> (choosing preimages of constants via `Classical.choose`).
