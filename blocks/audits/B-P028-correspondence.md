## Stage 1 -- read-back (fresh agent, Lean only)

> # Read-back of `B-P028.lean`
>
> ## Ambient setting
>
> Fix a type `S` of **sorts**. `A, B : SSet S` are sorted sets (families of carriers). `SortedMap A B` is a family of functions `f s : A s → B s`; `Sub A` is the family of sorted subsets; `SortedEqv A` is the family of sorted equivalences. `Sig` is a many-sorted signature; `Alg Sig` is a sorted algebra; `IsAlgHom Sig FA FB f` says the sorted map `f` is an algebra homomorphism. `IsElemTranslation`/`TlGen` denote elementary translations and their closure under identity and composition; `congCogenerated Sig A L` is the congruence cogenerated by a subset `L`; `sortedEqvLe Φ Ψ` means `Φ` is contained in `Ψ`.
>
> ## Definitions
>
> ### `pullbackEqv`
> For a sorted map `f : SortedMap A B` and a sorted equivalence `Ψ : SortedEqv B`, `pullbackEqv f Ψ : SortedEqv A` is **defined** by transporting `Ψ` back along `f`:
>
> > at sort `s`, `x ~ y` iff `Ψ s (f s x) (f s y)`.
>
> ### `inverseImage`
> `inverseImage f Y : Sub A` for `Y : Sub B` is the sortwise preimage: `(inverseImage f Y) s = f s ⁻¹' (Y s)`.
>
> ## Theorems
>
> ### `pullbackEqv_congCogenerated_le`
> Let `f : SortedMap A.1 B.1` be an algebra homomorphism (`hf : IsAlgHom Sig A.2 B.2 f`) and `M : Sub B.1`. Then
>
> > `sortedEqvLe (pullbackEqv f (congCogenerated Sig B M))
> >              (congCogenerated Sig A (inverseImage f M))`.
>
> In words: the **pullback along `f` of the congruence cogenerated on `B` by `M` is contained in the congruence cogenerated on `A` by the inverse image of `M`.** Concretely: if `f x` and `f y` are indistinguishable by `M` under all translations of `B`, then `x` and `y` are indistinguishable by `f ⁻¹' M` under all translations of `A`.
>
> Proof sketch. Given `x ~ y` in the pullback and a translation `V : A t → A s'`, the homomorphism sends `V` to a translation `U : B t → B s'` with `f s' (V z) = U (f t z)` for all `z` (fact `tlGen_map`). Membership in `inverseImage f M s'` unwinds via `f s'`, and the hypothesis applied to `U` yields the required biconditional.
>
> ### `congCogenerated_le_pullback_of_surj`
> Let `f` be an algebra homomorphism that is moreover **sortwise surjective** (`hsurj : ∀ s, Function.Surjective (f s)`) and let `M : Sub B.1`. Then
>
> > `sortedEqvLe (congCogenerated Sig A (inverseImage f M))
> >              (pullbackEqv f (congCogenerated Sig B M))`.
>
> In words: under surjectivity the **reverse** inclusion holds: the congruence cogenerated on `A` by the inverse image of `M` is contained in the pullback of the congruence cogenerated on `B` by `M`.
>
> Proof sketch. Given `x ~ y` in `congCogenerated Sig A (inverseImage f M)` and a translation `U : B t → B s'`, surjectivity lets one lift `U` to a translation `V` of `A` with `f s' (V z) = U (f t z)` (fact `tlGen_lift`); the hypothesis applied to `V` transfers the membership biconditional for `inverseImage f M`, which unwinds to the one for `M`.
>
> **Combined**: for a surjective algebra homomorphism `f`, `pullbackEqv f (congCogenerated Sig B M) = congCogenerated Sig A (inverseImage f M)` (the two inclusions are proved in the two theorems above; the equality itself is stated in `B-R021.lean`).
>

>

## Stage 2 -- comparison (fresh agent, read-back + contract only)

> # B-P028 verdict
>
> Contract: for a homomorphism `f : A → B` and `M ⊆ B`, `(f×f)^{-1}[Ω^B(M)] ⊆ Ω^A(f^{-1}[M])`; if `f` is an epimorphism, equality.
>
> Clause-by-clause:
> - `(f×f)^{-1}[Ω^B(M)]` ↔ Lean `pullbackEqv f (congCogenerated Sig B M)`: `pullbackEqv f Ψ` relates `x,y` iff `Ψ s (f s x) (f s y)`. Match.
> - `f^{-1}[M]` ↔ Lean `inverseImage f M` (sortwise preimage). Match.
> - First inclusion ↔ Lean `pullbackEqv_congCogenerated_le`, under `hf : IsAlgHom Sig A.2 B.2 f`. Match (hypothesis is exactly "`f` a homomorphism").
> - Equality under epimorphism ↔ the reverse inclusion `congCogenerated_le_pullback_of_surj` (requires `hsurj : ∀ s, Function.Surjective (f s)`) combined with the first, giving equality by antisymmetry (the read-back notes the equality itself is stated in `B-R021.lean`). An epimorphism of `Alg(Σ)` is a sortwise-surjective homomorphism, so the hypothesis matches. Match.
>
> Contract clauses with no Lean counterpart: none
> Verdict: equivalent — both the unconditional inclusion and the epimorphism equality (via the two inclusions over the two files) are formalized with matching hypotheses.
